3D Tetrahedron Domain: Verified Crouzeix-Raviart Eigenproblem

This report records the interval assembly and verified discrete eigenvalue solve for the Dirichlet Laplacian on the fundamental tetrahedron domain described in special_tetrahedron.md.

Domain

The benchmark domain is the fundamental tetrahedron TF with vertices

P0 = (0, 0, 0),
P1 = (0, 0, 1),
P2 = (1/2, 1/2, 1/2),
P3 = (-1/2, 1/2, 1/2).

Its exact volume is |TF| = 1/12. The point coordinates are binary rationals, so they are exactly represented in Float64 and become degenerate intervals when promoted by IntervalArithmetic.interval.

A single tetrahedron has only boundary facet DOFs for the Dirichlet CR space. The implemented benchmark therefore splits TF into four tetrahedra by adding the centroid (0, 1/4, 1/2), producing a nontrivial six-dimensional interior CR space.

The local CR basis on each tetrahedron is φi = 1 - 3Li, where Li is the barycentric coordinate associated with vertex i. Each basis function has unit average on its opposite face and zero average on the other three faces.

Assembly

The global matrices are assembled by create_matrix_crouzeix_raviart_3d. With T = Interval{Float64}, all local geometric operations and matrix entries are interval-valued.

MijK = ∫K φi φj dx
AijK = ∫K ∇φi · ∇φj dx

Reproduction

using VFEM, IntervalArithmetic

m = special_tetrahedron_mesh()
r = verified_cr_laplace_3d(m, 6)

println((m.NumNode, m.NumElt, m.NumF, length(r.dof.interior_dofs)))
foreach(enumerate(r.eig_value)) do (k, lambda)
    println(k, " ", inf(lambda), " ", sup(lambda))
end
